# Aftermath: City of Choices

**Aftermath: City of Choices** is a narrative-driven 2D RPG adventure game built in Unity.
Set in a post-apocalyptic world focused on reconstruction, you explore the city, meet survivors, and make choices that shape the community’s future.

## 📖 Introduction

In the wake of a cataclysmic event, society is attempting to rebuild. You play as a survivor navigating the complexities of this new world. Your decisions matter—not only for your own survival but for the fate of the city itself. Will you strive for peace, enforce a new order, or find another path?

The game features a branching narrative system where your interactions with NPCs, such as Maya, and your decisions in critical moments will determine which of the multiple endings you achieve.

## 🎮 Gameplay Features

*   **Narrative-Driven Exploration**: Explore the city, including key locations like the Community Center.
*   **Choice & Consequence**: Utilizes the **Fungus** narrative system. Your dialogue choices and actions trigger different story blocks and outcomes.
*   **Relationship System**: Your actions influence your standing with characters (e.g., Maya Favor system), unlocking different paths.
*   **Interaction**: Pick up items (like your Backpack) and interact with the environment to progress.
*   **Three Endings**: Reach one of three major endings based on your decisions.

## 🚀 How to Play

### Option A: Play Online

Open this link in your browser:

https://henrylok0.github.io/Aftermath-City-of-Choices/

### Option B: Run in Unity (Developer)

1.  **Clone the Repository**:
    ```bash
    git clone https://github.com/yourusername/Aftermath-City-of-Choices.git
    ```
2.  **Open in Unity**:
    *   Launch Unity Hub.
    *   Add the project folder.
    *   Open the project using Unity version **`2022.3.62f2c1`**.
3.  **Start the Game**:
    *   Navigate to `Assets/Scenes`.
    *   Open the starting scene: `MainMenu.unity`.
    *   Press the **Play** button in the editor.

## 📂 Project Structure

*   `Assets/Scripts`: Contains core game logic (`GameManager`, `PlayerInteract`, `PlayerMovement`) and narrative scripts.
*   `Assets/Scenes`: Contains the different game levels and ending variations.
*   `Assets/Animations`: Player and object animations.
*   `Assets/Sprites`: Game art assets.
